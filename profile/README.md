## About This Organization

This organization (STAGE-lab) serves as the central hub for our primary research, experimental codebases, data repositories, and open-source releases associated with our lab's publications.

## Our Ecosystem

We maintain two distinct spaces on GitHub to separate our cutting-edge research from our academic initiatives:

- [STAGE edu](https://github.com/STAGE-edu): Our academic branch.
