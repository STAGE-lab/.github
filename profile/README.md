Welcome to the official GitHub page for the **Spatial-Temporal Architecture of Geometric Energy (STAGE) lab**. 
We are a research group dedicated to push the boundaries of current technology and engineer the scientific realities of tomorrow.

## About This Organization

This organization serves as the central hub for our primary research, experimental codebases, data repositories, and open-source releases associated with our lab's publications.

## Our Ecosystem

We maintain two distinct spaces on GitHub to separate our cutting-edge research from our academic initiatives:

- [`STAGE lab`](https://github.com/STAGE-lab): Our research branch. (You are here.)

- [`STAGE edu`](https://github.com/STAGE-edu): Our academic branch.
